# 1. Record architecture decisions

Date: 2026-10-25 · Status: accepted

## Context

Decisions without a recorded reason are re-litigated or reversed by accident, by people and by agents.

## Decision

Significant decisions are recorded as short ADRs in `docs/adr`, numbered, never edited after acceptance
(superseded by a new ADR instead). Use the `/add-adr` command.

## Consequences

A small cost per decision; a large saving when someone asks "why is it like this".
